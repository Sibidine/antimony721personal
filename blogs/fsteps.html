Five Steps

Home Blog

A staircase of five steps. One knows not what lies beyond the threshold of the fifth, for they shroud themselves in the mist of misbelief despite appearing mundane. The only reason to climb this staircase is when you have an ailment you wish to heal, bouyed by the belief that beyond it lies a better place. A belief propagated by those who have walked past that threshold before.

I take the first step. Everyone who has lived long enough has made it here. The only action needed to take this step is deny the existence of their scar entirely. "I don't have a scar, it doesn't exist at all! There's no pain, no agony, how could I ever feel these if I wasn't hurt at all? There's no possibility of there even being any wound that could administer any pathos to me at all!" Delusion might an effective drug at inducing docility, but sooner rather than later it's dosage becomes insufficient, and attempting to overdose on this drug turns the docile into the deranged. Digression aside, my destination lies four steps beyond, and my endeavours must go on.

Notwithstanding those who are non compos mentis, most make the climb to the second step. It's similar to the previous in more aspects than previously thought. The only real difference is a change in the drug being administered: fury instead of fantasy. "How dare this scar exist? How dare Life draw this arrow of agony from its quiver of consequences and aim it at my beating heart? Oh, the gall of those sadistic souls who lay in wait till my guard had been broken, and whose targeted ambush at the site of my infliction delayed the haemostat of time from healing the wound! And where were my allies in my hour of need, whose ailments I assuaged at their time of affliction?" Moving past this step is a far tougher proposition than the previous- human beings are social creatures, feeling creatures. An affliction of the heart caused by Life, succeeded by scimitars of sadism cutting off any scions of silver linings is very difficult to forgo without acrimony . The efforts needed to take the next step requires a compartmentalisation of what makes us human- building non-porous walls around all the feels, and collate all that is cold, calculating and unfeeling in you to look past these digressions, douse the fire brewing in the annals in your heart, and persuade your body to push forth with the hope of finding the cure.

Not the biggest deal to most people. A healthy option, yes — but far from the groundbreaking, awe-inspiring ignitions that spark the most marketable of fairytales. Yet this unassuming product of rice bore both the whip of regret and the outstretched arm of redemption — a merciless devil driving sinners for eternity in the inner circles of hell, but also a Greek goddess who had returned to find her temple in disarray, yet made it her abode, willing to give all her neglectors a chance at redemption. A stark reminder of the deeds that left me in the pit I dug myself, and a rope dangled down to me in a bottomless well — a chance to make things better, if not right.

The third step is the entire journey of the train of thought. This is where the mind attempts to find a cure for the heart purely by reason. "If I don't expose my heart to some of those who I previously cared for, I won't be hurt again. Just shut oneself in! The depth of the wound surely cannot be that high anymore? That definitely solves this grievance of the heart!". The aforementioned also bear similarities to the first layer- the crudeness of delusion is merely masked by the facade of facetiously fabricated flights of thought. These facades however, are enough to entangle many a wounded soul. Their sisyphean arguments with the brick wall of reality cease not, for they mistake it's unfeeling silence as tacit admissions of correctness in their arguments. It's not difficult for this train of thought to go off the rails of reason and logic, and stray back into the land of delusion. Keeping this train on the tracks is indeed very difficult, since soon into the ride, all those of sound mind can see where the final stop is.

The next step is the final stop of this train of thought, where the tracks of reason end. There is no company to be found here, and thus none to shield you from the blow the discovery and eventual admission of reality deals. Delusion, fury and argumentation having all been left behind, desolation takes hold as one is left only with the undeniable in front of them.

Absolute zero is a theoretical temperature where all motion ceases to happen. Whilst not quite this extreme, the burden of reality weighs heavy on each sinew of the body and mind, so much so that the next step that appeared innocous now looks insurmountable. To make it to the fifth step and thus see beyond its threshold, grit and strength of heart are essential, such that the gravity of reality may be stomached, digested, assimilated and made peace with. And this step proved one step too far for me.

To make that final step, to climb the altar of acceptance, is to accept the reality and move on. Yet, my mind, which has stood by my side in my journey till now, deserts me, for it cannot distinguish between acceptance and forfeiture. Accepting the reality is an admission of mediocrity, a moniker of surrender. Mayhap it is my view of life, sculpted by the chisels of those who've mattered most to me, but mediocrity and stagnation is something I cannot bring myself to accept: neither in myself, nor in those who I hold close.

Alas, this rigidity of my mind and its refusal to go silently into the night burdens me beyond being able to make it to the fifth step. And thus, much like the progenitor of this wound and many of my own misdeeds, this is where my journey ends, right at the cusp of making it to the other side, at the very edge of success, at the second to last hurdle.

Or so it should. The scar has now metastasized, turning into a tumour inseparable from the flesh it is bound to. And to truly rid myself of this agony I no longer wish to feel, merely separating the tumour would no longer be possible. Thus, as I climb back down the steps, i separate from myself this pound of flesh, this piece of my heart this tumour had bound itself and become indistinguishable from. To shield myself from this agony, it became necessary to end its source from its very roots, even if that meant severing the xylem of pleasure too.

And thus, I bid adieu to this unrequited love of more than a decade, and pen the closing of this chapter.
